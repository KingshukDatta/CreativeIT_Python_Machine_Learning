Tips for Jupyter Markdown:
Fractions: Use \frac{1}{p} if you want a stacked fraction instead of 1/p.
Subscripts: Use the underscore _ (e.g., A_i).
Summation: Use \sum_{i=1}^{n} to get the limits above and below the sigma symbol.
Brackets: Using \left( and \right) ensures the parentheses automatically resize to fit the height of the summation inside.
